Someone has run a camera down your pipe and found a crack or a root mass, and now you need to know what comes next. Sewer line repair means fixing or replacing the buried pipe that carries waste from your house to the city main, by patch, liner, or new run. We do this for homeowners in American Canyon, CA, and our line is answered 24/7 when sewage is already coming up.

The sections below start with what to do in the first few minutes of a backup, then walk through what each finding usually leads to. After that you will see how the three repair types compare, what drives the price, and where the city's sewer code comes in.

Active problem

Sewage is rising in a tub, shower, or floor drain right now

.

When the main line is blocked or broken, every drain below the blockage becomes an overflow point. The lowest fixture in the house usually fails first, so a basement or ground-floor shower is the early warning.

  1. Stop all water use at once. No flushing, no laundry, no dishwasher, no running faucets..
  2. Keep children and pets out of the wet area, since sewage carries bacteria..
  3. Move rugs, boxes, and electronics away from the affected drain if you can do it without stepping into standing water..
  4. Take photos of any damage before cleanup, in case you file an insurance claim..
  5. Call (855) 575-2890 and tell the dispatcher which fixtures are backing up and which still drain..

We found a crack or root intrusion, now what

.

Roots do not break a healthy pipe. They find a joint that has shifted or a hairline crack, push in looking for moisture, and then widen the opening as they grow. Cutting the roots clears the flow, but the opening stays, so the roots come back.

That is why a repair conversation starts with where the damage sits and how much of the pipe it affects. One cracked joint near the house is a different job from a pipe that is cracked or offset in several places.

If you only have a verbal report from another plumber, ask for the video or at least the footage notes. A camera inspection of the sewer lateral shows the distance from the cleanout, so we can match the repair to the exact spot.

A belly cannot be fixed by lining. A liner follows the pipe's shape, and that includes the dip.

Spot repair, lining, or replacement: the honest trade-offs

.

A spot repair digs down to the damaged section, cuts it out, and splices in new pipe. It is the least expensive path when the damage is short and the rest of the pipe is in good shape. The downside is that you are betting the remaining pipe holds up, and the next failure may be a few feet away.

Lining, often called trenchless pipe lining, pushes a resin-soaked sleeve through the existing pipe and cures it into a new pipe inside the old one. It avoids trenching across the yard or driveway and suits pipes that are cracked or root-prone but still round and aligned. It does not fix a belly, a collapse, or a pipe that has separated, and it slightly reduces the inside diameter.

Full replacement removes the old pipe and installs a new run, either by open trench or by pulling a new pipe through the old path. It costs the most and disrupts the most, but it ends the question for the whole run. Choose it when the pipe is failing in many places or is no longer sloped correctly.

  • Spot repair suits short damage with sound pipe on both sides..
  • Lining suits cracks and roots in a pipe that is still straight..
  • Replacement suits bellies, collapses, and pipes failing along their length..

Lining a pipe that runs under the slab

.

Homeowners ask whether an epoxy liner can fix a sewer line under a concrete floor without breaking the floor. Sometimes it can, if the camera shows a pipe that is intact and aligned and we can reach both ends through a cleanout or an exterior access point.

It is not a fit for every slab line. A collapsed section, a bad offset, or a pipe with no access point may leave tunneling through the slab or rerouting the line as the only real fix. We will tell you which one applies after seeing the footage.

What makes one sewer repair cost more than another

.

Two sewer repairs on the same street can differ a lot, because the buried pipe and what sits above it are different.

Method

A short spot repair, a liner, and a full replacement are different scales of work, and the choice depends on the camera findings.

Depth and length

A deeper pipe means more digging and shoring, and a longer damaged stretch means more pipe and labor.

What sits on top

Concrete, a driveway, landscaping, or a slab raises the work needed to reach and restore the area.

Access

A working cleanout at the right spot can save real time, while a missing one means making a new access point.

Permit and inspection

If the city requires a permit, the fees and any inspection timing become part of the job.

Policies differ. Many cover sudden and accidental damage but not slow leaks, root damage, or worn-out pipe, and some exclude the buried lateral entirely. Ask your insurer what your policy says, and photograph any water or sewage damage before cleanup.

The city's sewer code and what it means before you dig

.

Title 14 of the American Canyon Municipal Code covers sewer and sanitation. Section 14.12.090 requires a city permit before anyone installs, alters, or repairs a public sewer, a side sewer, or a building lateral, and the permit fees get paid.

Because the code does not tell you in plain words which part of the lateral you own, call the city before digging and ask where your responsibility stops. Doing that first prevents the awkward case where you pay for a repair on a section the city maintains, or start work without the permit. For anything else about the permit, ask the City of American Canyon building division.

Septic tanks: what we do not do

.

We do not service septic tanks, pump them, or repair drain fields. If your property has one, you need a septic company for that part of the system.

The city has a sewer code, so if you are not sure whether your house connects to the city system, ask the city and check your property records. What we can do is repair or replace the pipe that carries waste from the house toward the tank or the main.

When the line keeps clogging and a repair is the better answer

.

A line that needs sewer line cleaning every few months is telling you something. Each cleaning gives you relief but leaves a damaged pipe in the ground, and roots regrow into the same crack.

If clearing buys you less time each round, get the pipe scoped and price a repair against another year of service calls. A wet patch in the yard may also come from the water supply side instead, which is a different job covered under burst pipe repair.
