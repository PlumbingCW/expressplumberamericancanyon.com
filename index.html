First five minutes

What to do while you wait for the plumber

.

The first few minutes decide how much damage a leak does. These steps are safe for any homeowner and help whatever the cause.

  1. Shut off the main water valve. In many American Canyon homes it is on the street side of the house or where the line enters the garage..
  2. Turn off power to wet areas if you can reach the panel without standing in water..
  3. Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house so the pipes drain..
  4. Move rugs, electronics and papers away from the water, and photograph the damage for your insurer..
  5. Call (855) 575-2890 so we can dispatch a plumber while you work..

If you smell gas, skip all of this. Leave the building, call 911 from outside, then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000.

What American Canyon water means for your plumbing

.

The City of American Canyon treats and distributes your water, buying nearly all of its supply from the State Water Project through the North Bay Aqueduct. The 2023 water quality report lists average hardness of 92 ppm, which is about 5.4 grains per gallon. That is moderate, so scale builds slowly, but tank and tankless water heaters still collect it over the years and benefit from a flush.

The city uses chlorine as its disinfectant, and the report found no lead in the 30-plus homes tested in 2021. A few addresses near the Vallejo line are served by the Vallejo system instead, so check your water bill if you are not sure who supplies you. If you suspect a leak in the city main, the city takes reports at (707) 647-4364, and after hours at (707) 995-8674.

Water heaters and earthquake bracing

.

The region felt the August 24, 2014 South Napa earthquake, with an epicenter about 3.7 miles northwest of American Canyon. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. If your heater stands loose in a garage, ask us to check the straps when we look at a new installation or a repair.
