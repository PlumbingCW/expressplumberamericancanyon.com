About American Canyon Express Plumbing

.

A phone-first plumbing service for American Canyon, built around one idea: you should be able to reach a real person at any hour.

(855) 575-2890

American Canyon Express Plumbing is an emergency and repair plumbing service at 5555 Broadway in American Canyon, California. We answer one phone line, (855) 575-2890, around the clock. Everything starts with that call, because plumbing trouble rarely arrives at a convenient hour.

We serve American Canyon and nearby towns, including Vallejo, Napa and Fairfield. When you call, we ask what you see, hear and smell, and we tell you what to shut off while a licensed plumber is dispatched to your address.

How we work

.

There is no online form on this website, and that is deliberate. A leaking ceiling or a backed-up sewer line needs a conversation, not a ticket number. You call, you describe the problem, and we decide together whether it needs an emergency visit or a same-day appointment when one is available.

After the plumber has looked at the problem, you get a clear price before work starts. If the job is bigger than the first call suggested, you hear about it before anything is opened, replaced or dug up.

What licensed and insured means for you

.

Plumbing contractors in California are licensed through the Contractors State License Board. You do not have to take our word for it, or anyone else's. The Board's License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov lets you look up any contractor in a minute.

Insurance matters just as much. A pipe job can damage drywall, flooring and cabinets quickly, and you want a contractor whose coverage is in place before the first wrench turns. If you want to confirm details before a visit, ask when you call.

The work we take on

.

Most calls fall into a few families. Drains and sewer lines account for the backups and slow sinks. Leaks and broken pipes cover everything from a pinhole in a copper line to a cracked service line. Water heaters, toilets, faucets and garbage disposals round out the everyday repairs. You can browse every one of them on our plumbing services page.

We also handle larger planned work such as repiping, water heater installation and commercial plumbing calls. For the full list of towns, see the service areas we cover.

What we will not do

.

We will not promise an arrival time we cannot control, and we will not quote a price over the web for a problem nobody has seen. We also do not diagnose mold or health effects. If water damage has been sitting for days, we fix the plumbing and point you to your insurer and a qualified remediation specialist for the rest.

That honesty is part of the service. A short, accurate answer on the phone is more useful to you than a confident guess. Sometimes the right advice is to shut off one valve and book a same-day appointment instead of paying for a midnight visit, and we will say so when that is the case.

Homes we see in American Canyon

.

American Canyon is a young city. It was incorporated on January 1, 1992, and its population climbed from 9,774 in 2000 to 19,454 in 2010. By the 2020 census there were 6,334 housing units, and 74.9 percent were owner-occupied.

For plumbing, that means most calls come from owners of homes old enough for their original water heaters, fixtures and supply lines to be wearing out, and owners who want a clear price before they approve a repair.

Those homes also share the same water. The City of American Canyon buys nearly all of its supply from the State Water Project, and its 2023 report lists average hardness of 92 ppm, which is moderate. We bring that up on calls about water heaters and tankless units, because scale builds slowly and a periodic flush can add years to the equipment.

How to check us out before you call

.

Look up any contractor, including ours, with the License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov. Confirm the license is active and read the details it shows. Then ask the questions that matter to you. What will the visit involve? Who is responsible if something is damaged? When will you hear the price?

A good plumber is glad to answer all three. Write down the answers if you are comparing more than one company, and keep the photos you took of the problem for your insurer. If an answer feels vague, that is worth noticing. Our job on the phone is to be specific about what we know and plain about what we cannot know until the plumber sees the pipe.
