A drain that gurgles and empties slower each week usually fails at the worst moment, often with the washer running. Drain cleaning in American Canyon, CA means a licensed plumber removes the blockage from the pipe with a cable or a water jet, then confirms the line flows freely before leaving.

While you wait, stop sending water down the house. Active problem

Water is backing up right now

.

A backup tells you the blockage is complete, or close to it. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere, and it usually goes onto your floor.

  1. Stop using every sink, tub, shower, dishwasher and washing machine in the house, even if only one drain is acting up..
  2. Do not flush the toilets. If several fixtures are gurgling or backing up together, the clog may be in the main line and the toilets will be next..
  3. Pull rugs and anything valuable away from the low drains, and wear gloves and boots if water has come up on the floor..
  4. Skip chemical drain openers. They sit in a stopped pipe, burn skin on contact, and make the job unsafe for the plumber who has to open it..
  5. If you smell gas anywhere, leave the house, call 911, then call PG&E, the gas utility for most of the North Bay, at 1-800-743-5000..
  6. Call (855) 575-2890 and tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected and which ones still drain..

Which drains are stopped tells us where the clog is

.

One slow sink is almost always a clog in that fixture's own branch pipe. A washer that overflows while the kitchen sink gurgles points to a shared line. When the lowest tub or shower fills with water as you run a different fixture, the blockage is usually in a larger pipe that everything joins.

That pattern matters because it decides the tool. A short cable handles a branch clog in minutes. A shared line needs longer cable and a closer look, and a house-wide backup moves into sewer line cleaning. Telling the dispatcher what you see saves time at the door.

Kitchen sink clogs and why they come back

.

Kitchen drains clog differently from every other drain in the house. Cooking grease runs down hot and liquid, then cools inside the pipe and coats the wall. Coffee grounds, rice and food scraps stick to that coating, and the opening narrows a little with every wash.

For a kitchen sink drain clog, the plumber usually runs a cable through the trap and into the branch line until the blockage breaks apart or hooks out. If you have a garbage disposal, the cable may go in below it. A disposal that hums but will not turn is a separate repair, and the dishwasher drain hose often joins the same pipe, which is why a dishwasher can leave dirty water in the sink.

We can show you what came out of the line, and the answer is usually grease. The fix after cleaning is a habit. Wipe pans with a paper towel before washing, and put cooled grease in a can instead of the sink.

Tub and shower drains

.

Hair and soap scum build a mat just below the drain opening, and the water starts to pool around your ankles. A drain strainer and a plastic hair catcher handle most of this, and you can lift a visible clump out by hand with gloves. Going deeper than the first few inches is where a cable earns its keep.

If the shower backs up when someone flushes a toilet or runs the washer, stop treating it as a hair clog. That is a sign the shared line is blocked, and snaking the shower alone will not help.

When a slow drain is really a leaking drain line

.

Most drain lines sit inside walls or under the floor, where a crack or a bad joint can leak without making any noise. The clues are a stain that spreads below a bathroom, a musty smell that stays after cleaning, or a floor that feels soft near a tub.

A cable cannot tell you if the pipe is cracked, but a camera can. If we see damage on the video, you get a clear price before work starts for the repair, and we explain what the picture shows so you can decide.

  • A stain or damp spot under a bathroom that never dries.
  • Water in the crawlspace or garage after a shower.
  • A sour smell near a fixture that has been cleaned.
  • Soft flooring around a tub or toilet.

Storm drains and sanitary drains are different pipes

.

The sink, tub and toilet drains inside your house connect to the sewer, which is the sanitary system. The grates in the street and the gutter inlets belong to the storm drain system, and only rain belongs there. Storm drains flow untreated to local creeks, then the Napa River and San Francisco Bay.

That separation matters when you clean up after a backup or a clogged yard drain. Do not rinse sewage, grease or cleaning chemicals into a street inlet or gutter.

The City of American Canyon also treats leaves in gutters and inlets as a violation of Municipal Code 14.28, and Public Works vacuums storm drain inlets before the wet season, so keep yard debris out of them. Questions about storm drains go to Environmental Services at (707) 647-5325.

What decides the cost of a drain cleaning

.

There is no flat number that fits every clog, because the pipe and the blockage change the work.

Which drain it is

A fixture trap is a short job. A branch line, a shared line or the main takes longer cable and more time.

How far the blockage sits

A clog right under the sink is quick to reach. One far down the line may need access from a cleanout outside.

What the clog is made of

Soft buildup yields to a cable head. Roots or hardened scale can call for a different tool or a jetter.

Whether a camera look is needed

If the drain keeps clogging, a camera shows the pipe condition so you are not paying to clear it again next month.

After-hours dispatch

A night or weekend emergency call differs from a daytime appointment, and the dispatcher explains this on the phone.

Homeowner policies differ. Many cover sudden and accidental water damage from a backup but not a slow leak or a worn-out pipe, and some need an added endorsement for sewer backup. Ask your insurer before you file, and photograph any damage before cleanup begins.

Worth knowing before a cleaning

.

A cleaned drain is not the same as a healthy pipe. If a cable clears the clog but the wall is rough, cracked or full of roots, the blockage tends to return. Water cannot carry debris along a damaged pipe, so tell the plumber how many times you have cleared it.

A repeat clog is the point to ask about a camera look, and the honest answer is sometimes that jetting or a short repair is the better spend. When a house-wide backup appears, the visit shifts to the sewer line, and your toilet repair and installation needs, such as a toilet that will not stop running or flush, are a separate job we can take on the same visit.
