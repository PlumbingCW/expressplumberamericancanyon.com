How ZIP codes fit in

.

People often search by ZIP code, so each town page lists the codes we dispatch to. American Canyon uses 94503, and 94589 reaches into Vallejo, where American Canyon is an accepted mailing alias. Vallejo also uses 94591, 94590 and 94592 for Mare Island. Napa uses 94558 and 94559, and Fairfield uses 94533 and 94534.

A ZIP code only tells you roughly where a house is. Water supply and housing age matter more for plumbing, so the town pages explain which utility serves each area and what that means for repairs. Fairfield water is noticeably harder than American Canyon water, for example, while Napa homes sit near a river with a flood history.
